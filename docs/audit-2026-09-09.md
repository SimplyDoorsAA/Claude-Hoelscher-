The four data contracts hold everywhere: 3,133 money values are integer cents,
no not-offered cell became `0` or `""`, every priced row carries a
`priceBasis`, and all 712 ids are unique. Prices are internally consistent —
prefinished is never cheaper than unfinished and a double prehung is never
cheaper than a single, across every row.

## Fixed

### 1. A page reference was sitting in the glass field (208 wood rows)

`glazing` held `"P.6"`, `"P.42"`, `"P.46"` and so on for 137 mahogany and 71
knotty alder rows — a column slip from the original spreadsheet conversion. The
Quoter reads `glazing` as the glass type, so those doors advertised a page
number as their glass: it showed on the card, in the **All glass** filter, and
in the quote line.

The value moved to a new `priceSheetPage` field and `glazing` is now `null`
there. It was **not** merged into `catalogPage`: the two disagree on all 208
rows (`P.6` against page `8`), so they are references to two different
documents and picking one would have destroyed the other.

### 2. One glass, two spellings

`Clear Low-E` (9 rows) and `Clear Low E` (79 rows) are the same glass and gave
two entries in the glass filter. Normalised to `Clear Low E`.

### 3. `2 Panel Ach Smooth`

A typo for `Arch`. It split one mahogany door across two cards — 3068 under one
name, 3080 under another. Corrected; they are now one model with two sizes.

### 4. Sidelites were offered twice

The Opening step asks for sidelites, and the accessory picker offered them
again underneath. Adding one there put a second sidelite on the line at full
price. Sidelites are out of the picker; the opening is the only way to add one.

## Fixed — accessory compatibility

Every one of the 22 hardware items was offered on every door: barn strap kits
on a mahogany entry door, iron masks on a full-lite. `accessoryRules` in
`data/catalog.json` now says which accessories go with which doors, and the
picker hides the rest and says how many it hid.

| Accessory | Offered on |
| --- | --- |
| Barn Door Hardware (14 SKUs) | barn slabs only |
| Iron Mask — Standard, Balfour, Windsor | wood two-panel square and arch doors |
| Speakeasy Kit — Mahogany | mahogany two-panel doors |
| Speakeasy Kit — Knotty Alder | knotty alder two-panel doors |
| Dentil Shelf | Craftsman models |
| T-Astragal | a double opening |
| Mull Cover, Fat Boy Mullion | an opening with sidelites |

Everything else — clavos, straps, jamb legs, casing, brickmould, subsills, SDL
bars — stays offered on any door in its line, which is what the sheets imply.
The rules are ordinary catalogue rows, so widening one is an edit in the Price
Manager rather than a code change. **The two-panel list is the one to check
with Hoelscher**: it is what you told me, and the pattern currently opens on 58
model names across both woods.

## Improvements identified

### Done

- **A step with one possible answer settles itself.** Finish and Opening now
  join glass, size and grille: a barn slab that is only sold as a slab no
  longer asks how to hang it.
- **The picker says what it is hiding**, so a missing accessory reads as a rule
  rather than a bug.

### Recommended, not done

1. **Wood glass belongs in the Glass step, not the model name.** On the wood
   sheet the glass is baked into the description — `3/4 Lite - Flat Glass`,
   `3/4 Lite - Decorative Glass`, `3/4 Lite Iron Grille` — so they land as
   three separate cards, and the Glass question never appears on a wood door.
   Fiberglass already works the other way. Folding the suffix into a glass
   option would cut the mahogany list further and make both lines behave the
   same. It changes what a card is on the wood line, so it wants its own pass.
2. **A pre-configured speakeasy door is not connected to the speakeasy kit.**
   The catalogue prices both `2PSQ VG, SE + Wood + IM` as its own part number
   and an `Iron Mask - Standard` kit at $207. The app will let someone add the
   kit to the door that already has one. Linking them the way the iron grille
   is linked to its base model would prevent that.
3. **Two sidelites are always the same sidelite.** A door flanked by two
   different sidelites cannot be quoted as one opening today.
