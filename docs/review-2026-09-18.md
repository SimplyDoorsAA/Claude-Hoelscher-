# Code review, 2026-09-18

A full read of both apps and the engine, at the dealer's request. Everything
below was found by reading; each fix carries a test that fails without it.

## What the read found sound

- **All money goes through the engine.** `packages/pricing-engine/index.js` is
  the only place a price is computed. Both apps call `priceLine`, `priceQuote`
  and `applyMargin` and nothing else; there is no arithmetic on a price
  anywhere in either HTML file. The two judgement calls the rules file does not
  settle — margin-on-sell, and one prehang charge per opening rather than per
  leaf — are still isolated in `ASSUMPTIONS` at the top of the engine.
- **Integer cents end to end**, and `null` is never read as zero. The Price
  Manager parses dollar entry by string arithmetic; the quoter does the same
  for the one figure the office types, the labor line.
- **No ghost code.** Every top-level function in `index.html`,
  `quoter/index.html` and the engine is reachable.
- **The catalogue, not the code, carries the vendor's grammar.** Glass rules,
  SKU placeholders, sidelite pairings, the speakeasy programme, grille designs
  and the dentil shelf are all data, so a change Hoelscher makes reaches the
  app through `catalog.json` rather than through a code change.

## Four things fixed

### 1. The cost line took the keyboard away from price entry (App A)

`buildCell` wired `gridKeys` (Enter and Ctrl+Arrow walk the grid) and
select-on-focus at the foot of the function, but the money branch returns early
when `S.showCost` is set. So with **+ Cost** on — which is how a price update is
actually done — every money cell lost Enter navigation, arrow navigation and
select-on-focus, and each new figure had to be selected by hand.

Both listeners moved up to where the input is created, before any branch can
return. `tests/appa.test.mjs` drives the grid with the cost line off and then
on, and asserts Enter walks down the column and the price is selected in both.

### 2. A mistyped labor figure printed as nothing (App B)

`parseDollars` returned `0` for text its regex did not match, so `1500.555` or
`fifteen hundred` in the Labor field billed nothing — on a document the customer
signs, with no red field and no warning.

It now returns `null` for text that is not a money amount. `estimateTotals`
reports `laborInvalid`, the field goes red with an explanation in its title, and
the drawer's job-total box says so in words. An empty field is still a valid
zero. `tests/print.test.mjs` covers a good figure, two bad ones and a blank.

### 3. The dialogs did not hold the keyboard (App B)

The configurator sheet, the quote drawer and the lightbox are all
`aria-modal="true"`, but nothing kept focus inside them: Tab walked out of the
open sheet into the catalogue behind it. The sheet also rebuilds itself on every
answer, which destroys the focused button and drops focus to `<body>`, so the
next Tab started from the top of the page.

Added a focus trap over the three — innermost wins, lightbox over sheet over
drawer — and focus hand-back on close, so closing the lightbox returns to the
sheet and closing the sheet returns to the card that opened it.
`tests/design-gallery.test.mjs` tabs forty times in each direction and checks
focus never leaves.

### 4. The line chip assumed there would only ever be two lines (App B)

`switchLine` took `others[0]` — with a third price sheet it would jump to
whichever collection the catalogue happened to list first, without asking.

The "keep them or start fresh" question was factored out into
`confirmLeavingLine`, and the chip now puts the line gate back up when there is
more than one other line to choose between. With two lines nothing changes.
`tests/stress-ui.mjs` serves a catalogue with a synthetic third collection and
checks the chip asks rather than jumps, and that the quote survives the switch.

## Raised, not changed

- **Catalog page 42 rates Small Reeded privacy 7; the app shows 8.** Read
  automatically off the older wood catalog PDF. The swatches and ratings are
  regenerated from whichever PDF is on file, so a hand-edit would be lost on the
  next extraction. It resolves when the 2026 PDF is on file.
- **The estimate always prints Deposits as $0.00** and the amount remaining as
  the whole job total. That is right for a new quote and wrong for a revision,
  but nothing in the app records a deposit yet, so it is honest as it stands.
- **A quote number survives a page reload with the job details.** Changing the
  customer name without pressing Clear reprints the previous quote's number.
  Clear starts a new job properly; the risk is only in editing in place.
