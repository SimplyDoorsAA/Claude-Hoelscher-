# Full audit — both apps, pricing first

*Run 2026-09-10 against `main` at `3a7dd4a`. Requested before the office begins
quoting with these apps.*

Everything below was measured, not reviewed by eye. Where a number appears, the
script that produced it is named.

---

## Verdict

**The money is right. The paperwork is not.**

Every price the engine produces recomputes exactly against an independent
implementation written from `pricing-rules.json` alone — 14,088 price cells and
400 randomised whole quotes, zero disagreements. All 137 door cards reach a
price through the interface with no dead ends and no console errors.

Three things stand between this and an office using it, none of them arithmetic:
printing, a missing priced section, and an ungated cost view.

### App A — Price Manager

- Money is parsed by pure string arithmetic, never through a float.
- Integer-cents and null-vs-zero are validated before publish.

  byte-identical.

## 2. Blockers for office use

### B1 — Printing produces the app, not a quote  ·  **FIXED 2026-09-10**

The quote drawer has a **Print** button. Neither app contains a single
`@media print` rule.

Measured under print media: the header, the search box, the catalogue grid and
**60 buttons** all render. There is no date, no quote number, no customer name,
no company details, no terms.

An office cannot hand this to a customer. This was the single most important fix.

**Resolved.** Print now builds a document instead of showing the screen. Two come
out of one quote: the customer estimate — letterhead, quote number, each opening
in plain words, deposit, tax, labor, signature block, terms behind it — and an
internal order sheet with part numbers, spec and cost. In print media nothing but
the document renders: 0 buttons, 0 form fields. `tests/print.test.mjs`, 30 checks,
recomputes every figure on the estimate from the catalogue.

### B2 — Prehang adders cannot be added to a quote

`fiberglassPrehangAdders` (24 rows) and `woodPrehangAdders` (38 rows) are in the
catalogue, are priced, and the pricing engine already handles `kind: "adder"`.
The quoter's "Add to this opening" picker builds only **Hardware** and
**Components** groups.

62 priced rows, **$8,966.00 of list price**, are unreachable. Interior casing and
1×4 adders run $69.00–$400.00 of list per opening. Every prehung quote that
should carry casing is currently short by that amount.

### B3 — Cost view is one unguarded click on a public URL

The quoter is published on GitHub Pages with no authentication. The
Customer/Cost toggle has no gate of any kind. One click shows:

```
Prehang charge      $100.00
Crate & shipping    $120.00
Retail margin @ 40% $1,621.87
Total cost          $2,432.80
```

Anyone with the link — a customer, a competitor — sees dealer net cost and the
margin. Verified in the browser.

## 3. Decisions the office has to make (not bugs)

### D1 — Freight carries the margin  ·  **WITHDRAWN — the finding was wrong**

Raised on the assumption that the charge was a delivery service being resold. It
is not. The dealer confirms it is Hoelscher's charge to get the doors to the
SimplyDoors warehouse, and has nothing to do with SimplyDoors delivering to a
customer's house.

That makes it **landed cost on the material**, marked up exactly like the door it
arrives with. The app was already right; there was nothing to decide.

Two things follow, and both are done: the printed estimate never calls it
*freight*, and the banded crate charge prints as its own line — "Crating &
freight from the manufacturer" — because it is banded across the whole order
rather than sitting on a door, and folding it silently into Materials would leave
a customer unable to add the column up.

### D2 — The 0.48 net multiplier on non-door sections  ·  **RESOLVED**

**The dealer confirms 48% applies to everything on the sheet** — jamb legs, casing
and 1×4 adders, iron masks, clavos and barn hardware alike. The app was already
right, and this exposure is closed:

| Section | List total | Understated by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Components | $2,738.00 | $1,423.76 |
| Prehang adders | $8,966.00 | $4,662.32 |
| Hardware | $4,137.00 | $2,151.24 |

Recorded as dealer-confirmed on 2026-09-10. Hoelscher has still not put it in
writing, so `vendorConfirmed` stays `false` and `dealerConfirmed` carries the date.

### D3 — Jamb depth is recorded but never charged  ·  **RESOLVED**

**The dealer confirms a Hoelscher prehung price includes the jamb, at either
depth.** The app already records the depth and charges nothing, which is right.
No change.

### D4 — SDL bars are priced per lineal bar, not per lite  ·  **CONFIRMED, WORK OUTSTANDING**

**The dealer confirms Hoelscher applies the grid at the factory and bills $35 per
lite.** So the per-lite charge is the one that matters; the bar stock is only for
work done in-house. A simulated-divided-lite door is quoted short today.

Outstanding: an SDL step in the configurator — how many lites — at $35 each, read
as list (so $16.80 to SimplyDoors, consistent with every other row on that sheet).

## 4. Minor

- **An accessory count reads per door, but bills per door × quantity.** Four
  clavos on a line with quantity 3 charges twelve and displays "+ 4 ×". The money
  is right; the label should say "each".
- **41 of 137 cards show a drawn silhouette** rather than a photograph. 96 show a
  photo, 5 of those borrowed from a base door and labelled as such.
- **`speakeasyOptions` is not editable in App A** — it round-trips safely, but
  changing the speakeasy grammar means editing JSON directly.
- **Four speakeasy combinations Hoelscher never priced** (Circle Top 2 Panel with
  a Balfour mask) and **four misprinted part numbers** — both already shown in
  the app and listed in `docs/speakeasy-programme-2026-09-10.md`.
- **53 of 587 product rows carry no catalog page**, all Smooth Collection.
